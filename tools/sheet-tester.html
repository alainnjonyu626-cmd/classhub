ClassHub sheet tester

Checks that the website can read a Google Sheet, tab by tab.

  1. Upload classhub-master-template.xlsx to Google Drive and open it with Google Sheets (File › Save as Google Sheets).
  2. Share › General access › Anyone with the link › Viewer.
  3. Copy the sheet's link from the address bar and paste it below.

Nothing is saved or sent anywhere except to Google.
