FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ClassHub.

What is ClassHub?

A single web page for your class: announcements, timetable, deadlines, handouts and past questions, all in one link.

Is it free?

Setting up a hub costs 5,000 FCFA per class, per semester. Viewing the hub is always free for students and parents.

Can I use ClassHub for my school?

Yes. A school can have only general classes, only technical or commercial ones, or even a single class. Anything you don't use, like past papers or the Parents' corner, can be switched off.

How do I get my class link?

Send us your class information on WhatsApp. Within 48 hours you receive a personalized ClassHub link to share in your class group.

Is ClassHub secure?

Anyone with the link can view it, so we never put private information on it, like results or individual fees. Share the link only with your class.

Where can I get help?

Message us on WhatsApp — you can reach us for anything about your hub, any time.
